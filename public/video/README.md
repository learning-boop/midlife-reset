# Hero media

`hero-movement.mp4` — silent 8s loop, 700x1050, 404KB, h264.
`hero-movement.jpg` — the same frame as a still, 34KB.

Source: Pexels video 8809592 (free commercial use, no attribution required),
cropped to 2:3 to match the hero column. A woman in her late 40s stretching,
green foliage in the foreground.

Chosen to match the site's own positioning: movement and capability, not weight
loss. The deck says "looking beyond weight alone" and "sustainable habits, not
short-term rules", so before/after or diet-programme footage would contradict
it, and a smiling subject presented warmly can read as a patient testimonial,
which is a regulated claim for a prescribing clinic.

Chosen for age: the subject reads late 40s to early 50s, matching the
perimenopause audience the hero speaks to. Most stock tagged "middle aged" is
actually 65+ (Pexels 6294930 is tagged that way and is clearly not), so check
any replacement by eye rather than by tag.

`HeroMedia.jsx` plays the clip above 1025px with reduced motion off and shows
the still otherwise, so phones never download the mp4.

Dr Sarika deliberately does not appear in the hero: her photographs carry the
About, Nutrition and Ready to begin sections.
