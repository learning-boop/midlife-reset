# Hero media

`hero-nutrition.mp4` — silent 7s loop, 700x1050, 343KB, h264.
`hero-nutrition.jpg` — the same frame as a still, 67KB.

Source: Pexels video 19343491 (free commercial use, no attribution required),
shot natively in portrait and cropped to 2:3 to match the hero column. Overhead
still life, slow drift. No people in frame.

`HeroMedia.jsx` plays the clip above 1025px with reduced motion off, and shows
the still otherwise — phones never download the mp4. Both paths give the same
composition, so the hero looks the same either way.

Dr Sarika deliberately does not appear in the hero: her photographs carry the
About, Nutrition and Ready to begin sections, and a fourth was repetitive.

To put footage of her in the hero instead, replace these two files and keep the
names.
